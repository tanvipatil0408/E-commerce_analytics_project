Project Objectives :-
 1) Track overall sales performance and order trends.
 2) Identify top-performing product categories.
 3) Understand customer purchasing patterns.
 4) Analyze delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
 5) Build an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders.

Tools & Technologies :-
1) PostgreSQL
2) SQL
3) Power BI
4) Power Query
5) DAX
6) Data Modeling

Project Workflow :-
 -Imported and explored the e-commerce dataset in PostgreSQL.

 -Used SQL queries for data validation and analysis.

 -Connected PostgreSQL data to Power BI.

 -Performed data transformation using Power Query.

 -Created DAX measures and KPIs.

 -Built interactive dashboards for business analysis.

Key Metrics :-
 1) Total Revenue: 14.21M
 2) Total Orders: 98.67K
 3) Total Customers: 95.42K
 4) Average Order Value: 144.01
 5) Average Rating: 4.03
 6) n-Time Delivery Rate: 93.23%

Key Insights :-
1) The business generated over 14 million in revenue from nearly 99 thousand orders.
2) Health & Beauty emerged as the highest revenue-generating product category.
3) Watches & Gifts recorded one of the highest average order values.
4) Customer ratings remained consistently positive with an average score above 4.
5) Approximately 93% of orders were delivered on time, indicating efficient logistics operations.
6) São Paulo contributed the highest share of customer revenue among all states.
7) Revenue displayed steady growth over the analyzed period.
